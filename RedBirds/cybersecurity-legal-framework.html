Bangladesh Legal Landscape

Domestic laws and regulations governing cybersecurity services within Bangladesh — from criminal statutes to sector-specific central bank guidelines.

🛡️
Critical
Digital Security Act 2018
// DSA — Primary cybersecurity statute
Governs unauthorized access, hacking, data interception, and digital fraud. Penetration testing and vulnerability assessments must have explicit written authorization — without it, the activity may constitute a criminal offence under Sections 17–19. The DSA also criminalizes publishing or transmitting offensive or false digital content, which affects how you communicate findings.

Moderate
ICT Act 2006 (Amended 2013)
// Partially superseded by DSA
Sections 54–66 cover hacking, data destruction, and unauthorized system access. Still operative alongside the DSA for civil and commercial disputes. Relevant when handling client data or conducting system scans. All scanning and probing must be explicitly authorized in writing. Civil liability under ICT Act may run alongside DSA criminal exposure.

Critical
Bangladesh Bank ICT Security Guidelines
// Mandatory for banking sector clients
Bangladesh Bank (BB) mandates strict ICT security standards for all scheduled banks and NBFIs. If your clients are financial institutions, your services must align with BB's Guidelines on ICT Security for Banks and Financial Institutions (2015, updated). BB may audit third-party vendors — meaning you. Vendor agreements with banks must be pre-approved by their IT governance committees.

Emerging
Personal Data Protection Act (Draft)
// Pending legislation — monitor closely
Bangladesh does not yet have a standalone data protection law, but a draft Personal Data Protection Act is under review. Until enacted, data handling obligations flow from the DSA and ICT Act. If you handle data of EU or UK citizens, GDPR applies regardless. Build GDPR-equivalent practices now — getting ahead of this costs far less than retrofitting compliance after enactment.

Moderate
BTRC Licensing Requirements
// Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission
If your email security services involve intercepting, filtering, or routing telecommunications traffic, BTRC licensing may be required. ISP licenses and network operation licenses are governed by BTRC. Cross-border data transmission may require BTRC clearance depending on the nature of your service delivery architecture.

Foundational
Companies Act 1994 & Business Registration
// RJSC + Trade License + TIN + VAT
Your entity must be registered with the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies (RJSC). Obtain a trade license from your local City Corporation or Pourashava, a TIN from NBR, and VAT registration if turnover exceeds the threshold. Export income from IT services may qualify for tax exemption under NBR notification — verify annually. IT-enabled service exports require ERC from Export Promotion Bureau.

International Regulations

Cross-border legal exposure — these laws apply to you from Bangladesh the moment you serve clients or access systems in these jurisdictions.

🔒
Critical
GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation
// EU/EEA — Extraterritorial reach
GDPR applies if you process personal data of EU citizens — regardless of where your company is based. Fines reach €20M or 4% of global turnover. Key obligations: lawful basis for processing, data minimization, right to erasure, breach notification within 72 hours, and Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with all EU clients. Appoint an EU representative if required. Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) govern data transfers from EU to Bangladesh.
All Services
🇬🇧
Critical
UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018
// Post-Brexit — separate compliance required
UK GDPR mirrors EU GDPR but is a separate legal regime post-Brexit. If serving UK clients or handling UK residents' data, you need a UK representative and must follow ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) standards. Breach notification deadline: 72 hours to ICO. Mandatory DPAs with all UK-based clients. International Transfer Agreements (ITAs) govern UK–Bangladesh data flows.
All Services

Sector-Specific
HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act
// USA — Healthcare data
If any US healthcare organization is your client and you handle Protected Health Information (PHI) during vulnerability assessments or compliance work, HIPAA applies to you as a Business Associate. You must sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), implement technical and administrative safeguards, and report breaches. Penalties reach $1.9M per violation category.

Critical
PCI-DSS v4.0 — Payment Card Industry Standard
// Global — Payment data environments
If you conduct vulnerability assessments or compliance management for clients handling card payment data, PCI-DSS v4.0 applies. You may need Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) credentials. Penetration testing of cardholder data environments requires strict scoping rules, segmentation validation, and report formats specified by PCI SSC. Version 4.0 became mandatory in March 2024.

Critical
CFAA — Computer Fraud & Abuse Act
// USA — Applies to US-based systems you test remotely
The CFAA makes unauthorized access to computer systems a federal crime. Even from Bangladesh, probing or testing US-hosted systems without explicit written authorization creates CFAA exposure. This applies directly to portfolio security and vulnerability assessments targeting US infrastructure. Every engagement requires a signed Rules of Engagement (RoE) document before testing begins.

🇬🇧
Critical
Computer Misuse Act 1990
// UK — Extraterritorial criminal reach
UK's primary computer crime law. Unauthorized access to any UK-based computer system — even remotely from Bangladesh — is a criminal offence. Penetration testing engagements involving UK systems require documented written authorization specifying exact scope, IP ranges, testing windows, tools permitted, and emergency stop procedures.

Moderate
Export Control Laws — EAR & Wassenaar Arrangement
// USA/International — Cybersecurity tool controls
Certain cybersecurity tools — intrusion software, surveillance tools, cryptographic technologies — are controlled under US Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and the Wassenaar Arrangement. Using, importing, or reselling such tools may require an export licence. Verify every tool in your technology stack against the Commerce Control List (CCL) before deployment.

Contractual
ISO 27001 & SOC 2 — Framework Obligations
// Not laws, but contractually required by enterprise clients
Many enterprise clients — particularly in finance, healthcare, and SaaS — will require ISO 27001 certification or SOC 2 Type II audit reports before engaging. These are not laws but carry contractual force. Having these certifications signals credibility and unlocks regulated market access. Budget 6–18 months to achieve ISO 27001 certification depending on your current maturity.

04

Contracts & Documentation

In cybersecurity, your paperwork is your legal protection. Every document listed here transforms your work from a potential criminal act into a legitimate professional service.

01 / Must-have
Rules of Engagement
// RoE
The single most critical document for any assessment engagement. Must be signed before any testing begins — verbal authorization is not a legal defence under the DSA, CFAA, or Computer Misuse Act.
  • Exact IP ranges and domains in scope
  • Testing windows — specific dates and times
  • Emergency stop ("get-out") procedure and contacts
  • Tools and techniques explicitly permitted
  • Explicit written authorization from system owner
  • Out-of-scope systems explicitly listed
02 / Must-have
Master Service Agreement + Statement of Work
// MSA + SoW
The MSA governs the overall commercial relationship. Per-engagement SoWs define specific deliverables, timelines, and acceptance criteria. Never begin work without both signed.
  • Liability cap — limit to fees paid, exclude consequential damages
  • IP ownership of reports, tools, and methodologies
  • Termination rights and notice periods
  • Governing law and jurisdiction clause
  • Dispute resolution — arbitration preferred for cross-border
  • Force majeure and change-of-scope procedures
03 / Must-have
Non-Disclosure Agreement
// Mutual NDA
A mutual NDA must be signed before any engagement discussion begins. Vulnerability findings, client architecture data, and system configurations are among the most sensitive data your firm will ever handle.
  • Definition of confidential information (include findings)
  • Exclusions for publicly known information
  • Duration: 3–5 years post-engagement
  • Permitted disclosures — legal requirements, court orders only
  • Data destruction or return obligations post-engagement
  • Remedies for breach — injunctive relief clause
04 / Mandatory (EU/UK clients)
Data Processing Agreement
// DPA — GDPR Article 28
For any EU or UK client where you handle personal data, a GDPR-compliant DPA is legally required — not optional. Without it, both you and your client are in breach of GDPR.
  • Roles: controller vs processor clearly defined
  • Data types, categories, and processing purposes
  • Sub-processor list with prior written approval requirement
  • Technical and organizational security measures
  • 72-hour breach notification obligation
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for BD-EU transfers
05 / Strongly advised
Professional Indemnity & Cyber Liability Insurance
// PI / E&O Insurance
Enterprise clients will contractually require evidence of insurance before engagement. A Certificate of Insurance is not optional for regulated-sector clients. This is also your financial safety net if an assessment causes unintended disruption.
  • PI/E&O coverage for errors in security advisory services
  • Cyber liability coverage for breach claims arising from your work
  • Third-party claims arising from assessment activities
  • Regulatory fines defence coverage
  • Business interruption if client systems are disrupted
  • Coverage confirmation via Certificate of Insurance for clients

06

Immediate Priorities

Three actions that carry the highest impact-to-effort ratio — do these before everything else.

01
Get your Rules of Engagement template legally reviewed
This single document separates your work from criminal hacking under the DSA, CFAA, and Computer Misuse Act. Have a qualified lawyer review your RoE template before your first vulnerability assessment or penetration testing engagement. Every jurisdiction you operate in should be reflected in the authorization language. This is non-negotiable and costs far less than a criminal defence.
Do now
02
Build GDPR-equivalent data handling practices today
Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act is coming. EU and UK clients already require it contractually. Building data minimization, breach response, DPA templates, and deletion protocols now costs a fraction of what retrofitting compliance will cost post-legislation. Treat GDPR compliance as your baseline standard regardless of which jurisdiction your client is in — it signals maturity and opens enterprise doors.
Do now
03
Begin the ISO 27001 certification journey
ISO 27001 is not legally required, but enterprise clients — particularly in finance, telecom, and healthcare — are increasingly refusing to engage uncertified vendors. The certification process itself forces internal security discipline that reduces your own legal and operational exposure. Budget 6–18 months depending on your current maturity level. Start with a gap assessment to understand your baseline.
Start soon
